*Features for SafeSight*
-
1. Display (screen)
   - Display visual warnings
   - Provide visuals (model) of cars behind and in the blindspot
2. Audio and haptic alerts 
3. Basic parking assistance
    - Change the display on the screen to the rear view camera, and some basic warnings on distance from other objects
    - Dynamic guide lines 
4. Collision warning
   - Ex: You are getting to close to vehichle without enough braking, so it will alert you to brake more 
   - log any potential crashes and license plates
5. Lane departure warning
    - Continuously checks distance from each lane marker and alerts you when you the distance starts gradually changing (not a lane change)
6. Blind spot detection
7. Approaching vehicle speed estimation
   - Estimates how far a car is coming to judge whether they going dangerously fast
8. Real time edge processing
